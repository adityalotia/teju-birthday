## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an intimate, nostalgic luxury aesthetic tailored for a deeply personal, celebratory milestone companion. The experience marries high-end editorial book design with memory-forward scrapbooking—feeling like an exquisite linen-bound archival volume translated into modern Android craftsmanship.

### Visual Pillars
- **Warm Editorial Elegance:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke literary memoirs and heritage publications, paired with restrained, ultra-legible modern sans typography for effortless interaction.
- **Tactile Paper Surfaces:** Flat, clinical digital white is replaced by warm alabaster canvases, soft ivory card stocks, and subtle warm borders reminiscent of archival paper stock.
- **Nostalgic Warmth:** Earth-fired terracotta, champagne rose, and calming sage evoke aged keepsakes, pressed botanicals, and warm golden-hour memories.
- **Graceful Android Touch:** Native Android navigation and gesture structures softened through pill-shaped interaction chips, gentle bottom navigation docks, and organic transitions.

## Colors

The palette operates in a dedicated light canvas mode, relying on earthy mineral and botanical tones anchored in deep organic inks:

- **Primary (`#BD6B56` - Dusty Terracotta):** Anchors high-priority actions, interactive prompts, and unlocked memory milestones.
- **Secondary (`#C98A7D` - Champagne Rose):** Soft celebratory accents, secondary badges, and delicate interactive moments.
- **Tertiary (`#7E9F8E` - Muted Sage):** Provides organic equilibrium, used for reflective progress states, archival tags, and calming indicators.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F5` - Warm Alabaster):** The base background surface, providing a warm, non-glare linen texture for comfortable extended reading.
- **Surfaces:** `surface-ivory` (`#F5EFEB`) frames container panels and scrapbook entry cards. `surface-elevated` (`#FFFFFF`) highlights interactive overlays, scratch-off vouchers, and high-contrast dialogue sheets.
- **Typography Inks:** `ink-espresso` (`#2A2421`) delivers rich, legible authority for headlines and primary text without the harshness of pure black; `ink-charcoal` (`#5A524D`) softens secondary descriptors, timestamps, and captions.
- **Accents:** `accent-gold` (`#D4AF37`) provides delicate celebratory flourishes, seals, and milestone borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes literary serif artistry (`Playfair Display`) with clean, contemporary geometric ergonomics (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

- **Playfair Display:** Reserved for hero milestones, cinematic letters, entry chapter titles, and commemorative quotes. Its elegant thick-thin contrast imparts bespoke editorial quality.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Drives functional UI, milestone descriptions, navigational tabs, interactive hints, and timestamps. It maintains crisp legibility at small sizes across handheld viewport resolutions.
- **Editorial Flourishes:** `title-editorial` leverages italicized Playfair Display for secondary metadata such as location notes, chapter epigraphs, and contributor sign-offs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for native mobile ergonomics using a 4-column fluid system on mobile screens (<600dp) expanding to an 8-column layout on foldable and tablet devices (≥600dp).

- **Screen Margins:** Fixed at `1.25rem` (20dp) on standard handheld devices to maximize edge comfort and thumb accessibility, scaling to `2rem` (32dp) on larger viewports.
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence:** Memory cards and narrative diary entries stack with `space-md` (16dp) gaps. Major chapter breaks and chronological shifts use `space-xl` (32dp) to establish breathability and contemplative pacing.
- **Safe Area Anchors:** Bottom content containers must reserve 96dp bottom padding to guarantee effortless clearance above the floating native navigation bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews stark digital drop shadows in favor of soft, diffused, warm-tinted paper layering and delicate borders:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Warm Alabaster (`#FBF9F5`) flat background surface.
- **Level 1 (Card & Paper Surfaces):** Soft Ivory (`#F5EFEB`) with a hairline border (`1px solid #E8DFD8`). Shadow is diffused warm ambient: `0 4px 16px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Elevated Moments & Vouchers):** Crisp Off-White (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(189, 107, 86, 0.06)`. Subtle perimeter line: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` for keepsake cards.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Overlays):** Crisp Off-White (`#FFFFFF`) accompanied by a warm tinted scrim (`rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.40)`) and an elevation shadow: `0 16px 40px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates rounded corners balanced with full-radius pill silhouettes to maintain a gentle, approachable tactile feel:

- **Standard Cards & Panels:** Medium rounding (16dp / `rounded-lg`) mirrors tactile paper stationery and photo cards.
- **Interactive Elements & Badges:** Full-radius pill shapes (9999px) for buttons, progress pills, and category tags to encourage touch.
- **Polaroid / Scrapbook Frames:** Soft asymmetric inner borders (8dp to 12dp) simulating framed photo keepsakes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Pill-shaped button filled with Dusty Terracotta (`#BD6B56`), Crisp Off-White text (`#FFFFFF`), with an elevation shadow of `0 4px 12px rgba(189, 107, 86, 0.25)`. Active/Pressed state darkens to `#A85A47`.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill-shaped with Soft Ivory background (`#F5EFEB`), bordered with Champagne Rose (`#C98A7D`, 1px), and Rich Espresso text (`#2A2421`).
- **Ghost / Text Button:** No background fill; Rich Espresso text with an optional Warm Gold underline for narrative links ("Read Letter", "View Memory").

### Memory Cards
- **Scrapbook Card:** Built on Soft Ivory (`#F5EFEB`) with a 16dp radius, framed by a subtle border (`#E8DFD8`). Photos inside carry an 8dp radius with slight warm inner glow. Padding is consistently `space-md` (16dp).
- **Milestone Highlight Card:** Built on Crisp Off-White (`#FFFFFF`) with Level 2 elevation, accented by a 1px border tinted in subtle Warm Gold (`#D4AF37`) at 30% opacity.

### Chips & Badges
- **Milestone Chips:** Pill-shaped tags using Muted Sage (`#7E9F8E`) at 15% opacity with solid Sage text, rendered using typography level `label-caps`.
- **Category & Year Chips:** Champagne Rose (`#C98A7D`) at 15% opacity with Rich Espresso text.

### Form Inputs & Prompts
- **Text Inputs:** Outlined field on Soft Ivory (`#F5EFEB`) with a 12dp corner radius. Inactive border uses `#E8DFD8`; focused border activates to Dusty Terracotta (`#BD6B56`) with a 2dp stroke width. Text entry uses Plus Jakarta Sans `body-md` in Rich Espresso.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded circular selectors utilizing Dusty Terracotta when active, containing Crisp Off-White tick marks.

### Native Android Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Surface:** Floating docked bar resting 16dp above the device navigation bar, with a 28dp pill radius. Background is Crisp Off-White (`#FFFFFF`) at 95% opacity with an 8dp blur backdrop filter and a soft boundary (`1px solid #E8DFD8`).
- **Icons & Labels:** Selected item features a Dusty Terracotta (`#BD6B56`) pill indicator backdrop with Rich Espresso icon; unselected items sit in Muted Charcoal (`#5A524D`).

### Interactive Specialty Components
- **Scratch-to-Reveal Voucher:** Crisp Off-White card base topped with an overlaid Champagne Rose / Warm Gold shimmering textured mask that brushes away via drag gestures.
- **Cinematic Memoir Sheet:** Full-bleed bottom sheet with Warm Alabaster background, featuring Playfair Display headers, Rich Espresso body, and an archival signature accent.
